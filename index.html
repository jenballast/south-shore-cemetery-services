Who We Are

Honoring lives. Caring for memories.

"We celebrate the lives of those who have gone before us by helping you care for their final resting places."

We care for gravesites and headstones across the South Shore of Massachusetts. Distance, health, and busy schedules keep many families from visiting as often as they'd like. We go in their place.

That's where we come in. We tend to grave sites, monuments, columbariums, and memory gardens with dignity, honor, and respect — so your memories are honored for years to come.

What We Do

Our Services

Stone Cleaning & Restoration

Periodic cleaning is an investment in the longevity of your family landmark. Natural growth like lichen and moss adds visual weight but accelerates stone erosion over time. We use the same professional tools and cleaning formulas as the national monuments — safe for polished and natural stone alike.

Our process requires a minimum of three visits to clean, treat, and protect the stone properly.

Regular Site Maintenance

Cemeteries mow the lawn — they don't tend to the details. Leaves, sticks, and debris accumulate around foot stones and base edges throughout the year. We handle the careful housekeeping that keeps a grave site looking cared for and intentional, not just maintained by the grounds crew.

Seasonal Plantings & Décor

Every season brings a new opportunity to show that someone is thinking of them. Our seasonal planting program runs nearly year-round: cheerful spring blooms, summer color, autumn arrangements, and Christmas wreaths with removal included at the end of the season.

How It Works

What you can expect

You can't always be there. Our job is to make sure you never have to wonder whether the site is being cared for — so every service comes with proof, not just a promise.

01

Photos before and after every service

You'll receive dated photographs showing the site as we found it and as we left it. No guessing, no taking our word for it — you see exactly what was done, every visit.

02

Video calls available when you need them

If you'd like to see the site live, walk through a planting choice together, or simply be present in a small way from a distance, we're glad to set up a video call.

03

Honest guidance on what's needed

Some stones need three cleaning visits. Some just need the leaves cleared. We'll tell you which is which, and we won't sell you a service the site doesn't call for.

04

Respect for the cemetery's rules

Every cemetery has its own regulations on plantings, décor, and seasonal removal. We work within them, so nothing we place is ever removed unexpectedly.

Ongoing Care

Annual care plans

Most families don't want to think about scheduling a cleaning every few months — they just want to know the site is looked after. An annual plan handles it for you: a set number of visits across the year, photos after each one, and no calls to make.

Plans begin from a clean baseline

A full stone cleaning — our standard D/2 restoration — needs to be completed before an annual plan begins, and it's quoted separately. Starting from a properly cleaned stone means every plan visit afterward is upkeep rather than catch-up, which keeps the site looking its best all year. If your stone hasn't been cleaned yet, we'll handle that first and roll you right into the plan.

About the plantings

We choose annuals and hardy perennials suited to South Shore conditions (USDA zones 6b–7a) and to your specific site — low, tidy growth that won't obscure the stone, and drought-tolerant varieties that hold up between visits.

Spring: tulips, daffodils, and pansies for early color.

Summer: marigolds, zinnias, and vinca — heat- and drought-tolerant, blooming right through the season; low sedum or dianthus where a permanent, tidy planting is preferred.

Fall: chrysanthemums — the classic flower of remembrance — in the deep reds, golds, and purples of the season.

For heavily shaded plots, we lean on hosta, coral bells, or daylilies instead. Every planting follows your cemetery's own regulations, with seasonal décor removed on schedule.

Plan pricing depends on the size and condition of the stone, the number of markers in the plot, and the distance to the cemetery. Multiple stones in one plot are discounted, since we're already on site.
