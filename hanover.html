Serving Hanover

Care for the places that hold your memories

Hanover's burying grounds include stones that predate the town's separation from Scituate. Older markers here are often thinner and more fragile than modern granite, and the wrong cleaning method causes permanent damage — which is exactly why we don't use pressure washers or wire brushes.

Most cemeteries mow the lawn. They don't clean the stone, clear the debris from around the base, or put flowers out in the spring. That part has always fallen to families — and when you live an hour away, or a plane ride away, it quietly stops happening. That's the gap we fill.

What We Do

Services available in Hanover

Stone Cleaning & Restoration

We use D/2 Biological Solution — the same stone-safe formula used on national monuments — applied over a minimum of three visits. No pressure washers, no wire brushes, no acids. Those methods cause permanent damage to granite, marble, and slate alike.

Regular Site Maintenance

Leaves, sticks, and debris accumulate around foot stones and base edges year-round. We handle the careful housekeeping that keeps a site looking intentional rather than merely mowed.

Seasonal Plantings & Décor

Spring blooms, summer color, autumn arrangements, and Christmas wreaths — with removal included at the end of the season, in line with each cemetery's own regulations.

Common Questions

Hanover questions we hear most

Do you service Hanover Center Cemetery?

Yes. It's the main cemetery in town — established in 1727 on land granted by Scituate, at 41 Main Street, with roughly 6,700 memorials. It's where most of our Hanover work happens.

Are older Hanover stones safe to clean?

Yes, with the right method. Some Hanover markers predate the town's separation from Scituate and are thinner and more fragile than modern granite. Pressure washing and wire brushes cause permanent damage to stones like these, so we never use them.

Can you maintain the site year-round?

We can. Seasonal plantings run nearly year-round — spring blooms through autumn arrangements and Christmas wreaths, with removal included at the end of the season.
