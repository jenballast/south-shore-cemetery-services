This one-time restoration is quoted and billed separately — it sets the site off on solid footing, so every plan visit afterward is maintenance rather than catch-up.

## About the plantings

Plants are chosen for South Shore conditions (USDA zones 6b–7a) and the specific site — low, tidy growth that won't obscure the stone, and drought-tolerant varieties that hold up between visits.

| Season | Options |
|---|---|
| **Spring** | Tulips, daffodils, pansies for early color |
| **Summer** | Marigolds, zinnias, vinca (heat- & drought-tolerant); low sedum or dianthus for a permanent, tidy planting |
| **Fall** | Chrysanthemums — the classic flower of remembrance — in deep reds, golds, and purples |
| **Shaded plots** | Hosta, coral bells, or daylilies |

Every planting follows the cemetery's own regulations, with seasonal décor removed on schedule.

## Notes on pricing

Annual plans begin after a full stone cleaning has been completed; that initial restoration is quoted separately. Plan pricing itself depends on the size and condition of the stone, the number of markers in the plot, and the distance to the cemetery. Multiple stones in one plot are discounted, since we're already on site. A portion of every plan goes to local veterans organizations and historical societies — the yearly total is available on request.
