Serving Marshfield

Care for the places that hold your memories

Marshfield spans everything from 17th-century burying grounds to modern memorial sections, and the care each one needs is different. Older slate and marble require a gentler hand than polished granite — we adjust the approach to the stone in front of us.

Most cemeteries mow the lawn. They don't clean the stone, clear the debris from around the base, or put flowers out in the spring. That part has always fallen to families — and when you live an hour away, or a plane ride away, it quietly stops happening. That's the gap we fill.

What We Do

Services available in Marshfield

Stone Cleaning & Restoration

We use D/2 Biological Solution — the same stone-safe formula used on national monuments — applied over a minimum of three visits. No pressure washers, no wire brushes, no acids. Those methods cause permanent damage to granite, marble, and slate alike.

Regular Site Maintenance

Leaves, sticks, and debris accumulate around foot stones and base edges year-round. We handle the careful housekeeping that keeps a site looking intentional rather than merely mowed.

Seasonal Plantings & Décor

Spring blooms, summer color, autumn arrangements, and Christmas wreaths — with removal included at the end of the season, in line with each cemetery's own regulations.

Common Questions

Marshfield questions we hear most

Which Marshfield cemeteries do you service?

We work at Winslow Cemetery, Cedar Grove, Centre Marshfield, Chapel Cemetery, and Couch Cemetery. If your family's plot is somewhere else in Marshfield, ask — we can usually accommodate it.

The stone is from the 1700s. Is cleaning safe?

It is, with the right method. Marshfield has stones going back to the mid-1600s, and those are typically slate or marble rather than granite. We use a biodegradable, pH-neutral solution and let it work over multiple visits rather than scrubbing. Aggressive cleaning is what damages old stones.

Can you place flowers for a specific date?

Yes. Tell us the anniversary, birthday, or holiday and we'll schedule the visit ahead of it so the site is ready on the day that matters.
