Serving Rockland

Care for the places that hold your memories

Rockland's cemeteries serve generations of local families, and many plots hold several markers across multiple generations. Multi-stone family plots are some of our most common work — and they're also where our multi-memorial pricing makes the most difference.

Most cemeteries mow the lawn. They don't clean the stone, clear the debris from around the base, or put flowers out in the spring. That part has always fallen to families — and when you live an hour away, or a plane ride away, it quietly stops happening. That's the gap we fill.

What We Do

Services available in Rockland

Stone Cleaning & Restoration

We use D/2 Biological Solution — the same stone-safe formula used on national monuments — applied over a minimum of three visits. No pressure washers, no wire brushes, no acids. Those methods cause permanent damage to granite, marble, and slate alike.

Regular Site Maintenance

Leaves, sticks, and debris accumulate around foot stones and base edges year-round. We handle the careful housekeeping that keeps a site looking intentional rather than merely mowed.

Seasonal Plantings & Décor

Spring blooms, summer color, autumn arrangements, and Christmas wreaths — with removal included at the end of the season, in line with each cemetery's own regulations.

Common Questions

Rockland questions we hear most

We have several generations in one plot. How does pricing work?

Multi-stone family plots are some of our most common Rockland work. Additional memorials cost less than the first since we're already on site, and combining services across seasons brings the price down further.

Do you work at Mount Pleasant and Holy Family?

Yes, along with Old Town, Maplewood, Spring Lake, and St. Patrick's. Mount Pleasant also holds the G.A.R. Soldiers' Lot and the 1913 monument to the Unreturned Soldiers.

Do you offer anything for veterans' graves?

A portion of every service we perform goes to local veterans organizations and historical societies, and the amount is printed on your receipt. If you'd like flags placed for Memorial Day or Veterans Day, just ask.
