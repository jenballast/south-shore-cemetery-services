Serving Hull

Care for the places that hold your memories

Hull is surrounded by water on nearly every side, which makes it one of the harder places on the South Shore to keep a monument looking clean. Salt and constant moisture mean stones here typically need cleaning more often than those a few miles inland.

Most cemeteries mow the lawn. They don't clean the stone, clear the debris from around the base, or put flowers out in the spring. That part has always fallen to families — and when you live an hour away, or a plane ride away, it quietly stops happening. That's the gap we fill.

What We Do

Services available in Hull

Stone Cleaning & Restoration

We use D/2 Biological Solution — the same stone-safe formula used on national monuments — applied over a minimum of three visits. No pressure washers, no wire brushes, no acids. Those methods cause permanent damage to granite, marble, and slate alike.

Regular Site Maintenance

Leaves, sticks, and debris accumulate around foot stones and base edges year-round. We handle the careful housekeeping that keeps a site looking intentional rather than merely mowed.

Seasonal Plantings & Décor

Spring blooms, summer color, autumn arrangements, and Christmas wreaths — with removal included at the end of the season, in line with each cemetery's own regulations.

Common Questions

Hull questions we hear most

Hull is surrounded by water. How does that affect the stone?

Significantly. Salt and near-constant moisture make Hull one of the harder places on the South Shore to keep a monument clean. Stones here typically need attention more often than those even a few miles inland.

Do you work at Hull Village Cemetery?

Yes. It's the town's main cemetery, on Nantasket Avenue, with more than 2,400 recorded memorials — including the graves of local soldiers decorated each Memorial Day.

What's included in regular maintenance?

Clearing leaves, sticks, and debris from around the foot stones and base edges, trimming back growth encroaching on the marker, and keeping the site looking tended between cleanings. Cemeteries mow the lawn; they don't do this part.
